🥐 Classic Croissants

Makes 8 | Allow a day for chilling and proofing

Ingredients

  • 500 g all-purpose flour
  • 60 g sugar, 10 g salt, and 7 g instant yeast
  • 300 ml cold milk and 40 g softened butter
  • 250 g cold unsalted butter for laminating
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing

Method

  1. Mix flour, sugar, salt, yeast, milk, and softened butter. Knead until smooth, then chill for 1 hour.
  2. Enclose the cold butter in the dough. Roll and fold into thirds three times, chilling for 30 minutes between folds.
  3. Roll into a rectangle, cut into triangles, roll into crescents, and proof until airy, about 2 hours.
  4. Brush with egg and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 18–22 minutes until deeply golden.

🍫 Pain au Chocolat

Makes 8 | Use the croissant dough above

Ingredients

  • 1 batch chilled croissant dough
  • 16 dark chocolate batons or thick chocolate sticks
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing

Method

  1. Roll dough into a rectangle and cut into 8 smaller rectangles.
  2. Place a chocolate baton near one short edge, roll once, add another baton, and finish rolling.
  3. Proof seam-side down until puffy, brush with egg, and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 15–18 minutes.

🍯 Kouign-Amann

Makes 8 | A crisp, caramelized Breton pastry

Ingredients

  • 500 g croissant dough, after its first turn
  • 200 g granulated sugar, plus extra for dusting
  • 200 g cold unsalted butter, sliced
  • A pinch of flaky salt

Method

  1. Roll the dough into a square. Scatter with sugar and salt, top with butter, and fold into thirds.
  2. Roll and fold into thirds once more, then chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Cut into 8 squares, fold the corners toward the center, and place in sugared muffin cups.
  4. Proof until puffy and bake at 190°C (375°F) for 25–30 minutes until caramelized.

🍂 Palmiers

Makes about 20 | A simple crisp cookie

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet all-butter puff pastry, thawed but cold
  • 100 g granulated sugar
  • A pinch of ground cinnamon (optional)

Method

  1. Mix the sugar with cinnamon, if using. Sprinkle half over the counter and lay the pastry on top.
  2. Sprinkle the rest over the pastry. Fold both long edges inward twice until they meet in the middle.
  3. Chill for 20 minutes, then slice into 1 cm pieces and place cut-side up on a lined tray.
  4. Bake at 200°C (400°F) for 10 minutes per side, turning once, until crisp and golden.
